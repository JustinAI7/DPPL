DEPARTMENT OBJECTIVE

To elevate the standard of event execution within UNORSSGO by replacing archaic, paper-driven workflows with a zero-typing digital infrastructure. Our objective is to minimize administrative turnaround times, eliminate scheduling friction, and provide a seamless, click-and-select request portal that optimizes the organization and reservation of bookings, permits, and logistical assets for the student body.
